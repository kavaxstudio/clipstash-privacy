ClipStash · Privacy

Your privacy matters.

This policy explains how ClipStash handles the information you save and how its third-party services work.

App: ClipStash Package: com.clipstash.vault Effective date: November 2026

Your vault stays on your device. Links, payment details such as IBANs, addresses, quick notes, pinned items, categories, and 24-hour or 7-day auto-expiry items are stored locally. ClipStash does not operate a backend server or upload your saved vault items to a cloud database.

Information stored on your device

Everything you save in ClipStash—including links, payment details, addresses, quick notes, pinned items, categories, and expiry settings—is stored locally on your device.

We do not receive or have access to your vault items through our own systems because we do not operate a vault backend. This on-device storage description does not describe information that third-party services may process, as explained below.

How you control your data

  • Delete individual items inside the app.
  • Let 24-hour or 7-day automatic expiry remove items.
  • Clear ClipStash app data or uninstall the app from Android.
  • Manage ad and consent preferences where available.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes locally stored vault data from your device. These actions do not delete information already processed by Google services. For help with a deletion request, contact andrewtan.2011@gmail.com.

Third-party services

Google AdMob and consent controls

ClipStash shows a bottom banner ad and a frequency-capped interstitial ad. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides consent controls where required.

Depending on consent and device settings, Google AdMob may process the Android Advertising ID, IP address, and general device or ad interaction signals to show and measure personalized or non-personalized ads. Google processes this information according to its own privacy policy.

Read Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics collects anonymous, aggregated app usage events, including feature and category actions, copy/save counts, and general engagement. This helps us understand how ClipStash is used.

It does not log your note contents, URLs, IBANs, or addresses.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs, stack traces, and basic device or operating-system information when an error occurs. This helps us investigate and fix stability problems.

Security and retention

Your saved vault items are kept locally and are not uploaded to a cloud database operated by us. Network connections used by Google services use transport encryption.

Google services may retain their collected data according to Google's policies, retention settings, and applicable legal requirements.

Children's privacy

ClipStash is a general utility app. It is not designed to collect personal information from children through its own vault service. Third-party services have their own age and consent requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when necessary.
