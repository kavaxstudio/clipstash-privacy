ClipStash · Privacy

Your privacy matters.

This policy explains what ClipStash stores on your device and how the third-party services used by the app may process information.

App: ClipStash Package: com.clipstash.vault Effective date: November 2026

Your vault stays on your device. Links, payment details including IBANs, addresses, quick notes, pinned items, categories, and items with 24-hour or 7-day auto-expiry are stored locally on your device. ClipStash does not operate a backend server or upload vault items to a cloud database owned by us.

Information stored on your device

Everything you save in ClipStash—including links, payment details such as IBANs, addresses, quick notes, pinned items, categories, and expiry settings—is kept locally on your device.

We do not receive or have access to your vault items through our own systems: ClipStash has no backend server, and we do not upload those items to a cloud database that we own. This does not cover information that the third-party services below may process independently.

How you control your data

  • Delete individual items inside the app.
  • Let 24-hour or 7-day automatic expiry clear eligible items.
  • Clear ClipStash app data or uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • Manage ad and consent preferences where applicable.

Clearing app data or uninstalling ClipStash removes locally stored vault data from your device. It does not delete information already collected by third-party services. For questions or help with a request about data processed by ClipStash, contact support@clipstash.app.

Third-party services

Google AdMob and Google UMP consent

ClipStash displays a bottom banner ad and a frequency-capped interstitial ad. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides consent choices where applicable.

Depending on your consent choice and device settings, Google AdMob or Google may process advertising identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, your IP address, and general device or ad interaction signals to serve or measure personalized or non-personalized ads. Google's processing is governed by its policies and your consent choices.

Read Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics collects anonymous, aggregated app usage events, such as feature and category actions, copy/save counts, and general engagement. This helps us understand how the app is used.

Analytics does not log the contents of your notes, URLs, IBANs, or addresses.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs, stack traces, and basic device and operating-system diagnostics when a crash or error occurs. This helps us investigate and fix stability issues.

Security and retention

Your saved vault items stay on your device and are not uploaded to a cloud database operated by us. Network connections used by Google services are encrypted in transit. Information collected by third-party services may be retained according to their policies, settings, and applicable legal requirements.

Children's privacy

ClipStash is a general utility app. It is not designed to collect personal information from children through a vault backend. Third-party services have their own age and consent requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when necessary.
